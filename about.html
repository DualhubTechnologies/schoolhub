About Us

Built for the way Ugandan schools actually run

SchoolHubUG is a cloud-based school management platform designed to bring admissions, academics, fees, discipline and reporting into one secure system — so school leaders spend less time chasing paperwork and more time on learning outcomes.

Why SchoolHubUG

Every school office knows this problem

Admissions live in one register, marks in another, fee ledgers in a third, and discipline notes in a teacher's notebook. When a director, bursar, or head teacher needs a clear picture of the school, it takes hours to piece together — and the picture is often already out of date.

SchoolHubUG was created to close that gap: a single, secure place for a school's records that any authorised staff member can access from a phone, tablet, or computer, whether they're in the office or off-site.

What Drives Us

Mission & vision

Our mission

To simplify school administration in Uganda by giving directors, head teachers, bursars, teachers, and parents one reliable platform to manage academics, finances, and communication — improving accountability and freeing up time for teaching and learning.

Our vision

A future where every school in Uganda — from a small nursery to a large multi-campus institution — has clear, real-time visibility into its academic performance, finances, and operations, and can make decisions backed by accurate data.

What We Stand For

Our core values

Accountability

Clear, traceable records for every admission, payment, and mark entered — so trust between school, staff, and parents is easy to maintain.

Simplicity

Software that head teachers, bursars, and teachers can actually learn quickly, without needing a technical background.

Security

Role-based access and secure cloud storage, so sensitive student, academic, and financial data is protected.

Partnership

We work alongside every school we support — listening to real administrative challenges and building around them.

Who We Serve

Every level of Uganda's education system

  • Nursery schools managing early enrolment and parent communication.
  • Primary schools tracking assessments, attendance, and fees across classes.
  • Secondary schools handling report cards, discipline records, and larger staff teams.
  • Tertiary and vocational institutions coordinating multiple programmes and cohorts.

The Team Behind The Platform

About DualHub Technologies

SchoolHubUG is a product of DualHub Technologies, a Uganda-focused technology company building practical software for local institutions. We continue to develop SchoolHubUG based on direct feedback from the school administrators, bursars, and teachers who use it.
