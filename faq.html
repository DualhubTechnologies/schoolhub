Security, Training & Support

SchoolHubUG uses secure cloud hosting and role-based access control, so staff only see the records relevant to their role — for example, teachers, bursars, and administrators each have different levels of access to student, academic, and financial data.

SchoolHubUG is designed to be simple enough for non-technical staff to learn quickly. Guided onboarding and staff walkthroughs are available so your team feels confident using the system from day one.

Our team is reachable by phone, WhatsApp, and email for setup help and day-to-day questions, and we work with schools to resolve issues promptly.
