Is SchoolHub suitable for primary and secondary schools?

SchoolHub can be configured around different class structures, subjects, fees and reporting needs. We confirm the exact fit during your demo.

Can parents access SchoolHub on a phone?

Yes. The website and portal experience are designed to work on modern mobile devices as well as computers.

Does it support Mobile Money?

SchoolHub is designed with Mobile Money readiness. Available payment integrations, providers and any related costs are confirmed during setup.

Can you help us move our existing records?

Data preparation and migration assistance can be included depending on the format, quality and volume of your current records.
