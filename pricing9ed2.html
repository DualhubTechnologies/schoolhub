    What is a Sender Id?

    A Sender Id is a Unique name stamped on SMS messages being sent out to your SACCO Members. It is registered with Mobile Service Providers. For example, if your SACCO name is Kamwe Kamwe Traders Sacco Ltd., you can purchase that Sender Id and messages going out from your account as being from Kamwe1Sacco (with a limit of upto 11 characters).


    • With Sender Id, members will not be able to reply to that Sender Id. Also, note that Sender Ids are case sensitive.
    • Once you purchase a Sender Id, you will have the option of using it for messages originating from your SACCO.
